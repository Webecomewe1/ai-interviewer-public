    BEFORE YOU BEGIN

    A clearer way to prepare.

    What do I get after an interview?

    A transcript, AI feedback across six skill areas, suggestions, and a focused practice plan. Use the feedback to refine your answers; AI ratings are practice guidance and do not predict hiring outcomes.

    Do I need a microphone?

    Only for a voice session. Text interviews work without a microphone. If voice is unavailable, choose text practice. The sample warm-up works without an account or connection to the AI service.

    How is my information used?

    Your setup context is saved in this browser. Starting an AI interview sends your context and answers to the server and Google Gemini to generate questions and feedback. Signed-in sessions can be stored in your account. Use “Clear saved context” to remove the setup draft from this device; this does not delete account sessions.

    How should I use the feedback?

    Choose one area to work on, rewrite an answer using a specific example, and practice again. For behavioral questions, try Situation, Task, Action, Result. For technical questions, explain your approach and the trade-offs.
